\section{Experimental Design}\label{sec:experiments}
In our methodology, we introduced our model equipped with agentic architecture, counter-factual reasoning, and a new sub-group level scoring mechanism which effectively turns any foundation model into a tool for the agentic use. We experiment on the FairVision30K dataset and the Harvard GDP dataset. For FairVision, we test on Glaucoma, Age related Macular Degeneration (AMD), and Diabetic Retinopathy (DR). For Harvard GDP, we test Glaucoma, and progression of Glaucoma. We also test on external datasets like REFUGE2 with no demographic information.

RetinAgent is designed as clinician-facing decision support rather than an
autonomous replacement for the ophthalmologist. For each patient, the system
organizes the available imaging, model outputs, structural or functional
evidence, and reliability information into a concise case-level assessment.
The ophthalmologist remains responsible for interpreting the evidence and
making the final clinical decision, while uncertain or discordant cases are
explicitly flagged for review.
