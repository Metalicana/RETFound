\newcommand{\RetinAgentDiagnosticCaption}{\textbf{RetinAgent diagnostic workflow.}
Available demographics and retinal imaging supply patient summaries, visual
reports, RETFound predictions, cup-to-disc measurements and reliability lookup,
where configured. The counterfactual agent assesses dependence on individual
evidence sources. The ophthalmologist orchestrator receives both the original
case evidence and the audit to produce a diagnosis and rationale. Burgundy
circles denote language-model roles; teal symbols in the evidence panel denote
models and tools. OCT, SLO and CFP are examples of cohort-dependent modalities,
not necessarily examinations from the same patient.}

% Page 2: retinagent_progression.pdf
\newcommand{\RetinAgentProgressionCaption}{\textbf{RetinAgent for glaucoma-progression forecasting.}
Baseline RNFLT imaging, optional baseline OCT, visual-field total-deviation
values and demographics supply structural and functional reports. These are
combined with helper-model predictions and development-set reliability.
An evidence-ablation audit and the original evidence inform six endpoint
forecasts from the ophthalmologist orchestrator. Each forecast retains a
binary decision, rationale and review flag. Follow-up examinations are not
provided as inputs; review flags do not remove the forced predictions.}
