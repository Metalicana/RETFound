\caption{\textbf{Accepted-case performance under a saved-risk threshold sweep
in the historical deterministic fusion experiment.}
Macro-F1 among accepted cases is plotted against the percentage of cases
escalated to clinician review for (\textbf{a}) glaucoma, (\textbf{b}) AMD and
(\textbf{c}) diabetic retinopathy on Harvard FairVision30K (3,000 held-out
cases per task, at observed task-specific prevalence).
Saved reliability-weighted fusion predictions are held fixed, and cases are
accepted when their recorded risk score is at or below the swept threshold.
Equal scores are retained together; lines connect the resulting operating
points. Dashed segments mark accepted subsets with fewer than 20 positive or
20 negative cases; this is a display warning rather than a threshold-selection
rule. Single-class and empty subsets are not plotted. Points at zero escalation
give full-cohort macro-F1. Class composition changes with escalation and can
affect F1; accepted class counts and confusion matrices are provided in the
source data. These are descriptive point estimates, not uncertainty intervals
or evidence of clinical benefit. This post-hoc score sweep is neither a sweep
of the original joint conformal/escalation policy nor a result from the live
250-case-per-task RetinAgent evaluation.}
\label{fig:historical_fusion_escalation}
